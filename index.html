Traditional Yerba Mate 🧉

Yerba mate is a traditional South American drink wichim obsesed, especially popular in Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, and southern Brazil, where it is known as Chimarrão. It is prepared using yerba mate leaves and hot water.

  • Preparation Time: 5 minutes
  • Servings: 1 person,but in places like argentina ,mate is part of their cultural socialization they usually drink from the same cup and alternate bwtween their friends or family wild talking
Ingredients Quantity
Yerba Mate Fill 2/3 of the mate cup
Hot Water 70-80°C (158-176°F)
Mate Cup 1
Bombilla (Metal Straw) 1

Instructions

  1. Fill your mate cup about 2/3 full with yerba mate.
  2. Cover the opening and gently shake the cup to distribute the yerba.
  3. Tilt the cup to create a slope with the yerba.
  4. Add a small amount of warm water to the lower side.
  5. Insert the bombilla into the wet area.
  6. Slowly pour hot water (70-80°C) near the bombilla.
  7. Enjoy your traditional yerba mate!

Tacos al Pastor - Mexico

Tacos al Pastor are one of Mexico's most popular street in my home town. They are made with marinated pork, pineapple, onion, and cilantro. This dish combines Mexican flavors with cooking techniques influenced by Lebanese immigrants wich i have the theory that im probably be a desendant.

  • Preparation Time: 30 minutes + marinating
  • Cooking Time: 20 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity
Pork 500 grams
Achiote paste 2 tablespoons
Pineapple 1 cup
Corn tortillas 12
Onion and cilantro To taste
Orange juice 1/2 cup

Instructions

  1. Mix the achiote paste with orange juice.
  2. Cut the pork into thin slices and coat with the marinade.
  3. Refrigerate the pork for at least 2 hours.
  4. Cook the pork thoroughly in a hot pan.
  5. Cut the cooked pork into small pieces.
  6. Warm the corn tortillas.
  7. Fill the tortillas with pork, pineapple, onion, and cilantro.
  8. Serve with your favorite salsa and lime.

Traditional Arepas - Venezuela & Colombia

Arepas are a traditional food from Venezuela and Colombia.My mom actually give me this receipe from her venezuelan friends . arepas are made with corn flour, water, and salt. Arepas can be grilled, baked, or fried and are often served with cheese, meat, beans, or avocado.

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 15-20 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity
Pre-cooked corn flour 2 cups
Warm water 2 1/2 cups
Salt 1 teaspoon
Vegetable oil 1 tablespoon
Cheese or avocado Optional

Instructions

  1. Pour the warm water into a large bowl.
  2. Add the salt and mix well.
  3. Slowly add the pre-cooked corn flour.
  4. Mix until a soft dough forms.
  5. Let the dough rest for 5 minutes.
  6. Divide the dough into equal portions.
  7. Shape each portion into a round, flat disc.
  8. Heat a pan with a little vegetable oil.
  9. Cook the arepas for 7-10 minutes on each side until golden.
  10. Serve with cheese, avocado, or your favorite filling.
